(04) — Safety & compliance

Safety is not a department. It’s the company.

  • Audits & training. Safety audits, meetings, surveys, awareness and training programmes conducted on a regular basis across the fleet.
  • Statutory compliance. Compliance with all safety codes and statutory requirements laid down by the Central & State Governments.
  • People & environment first. Protecting our workforce and the environment from the hazards of handling LPG, gases, oils, ACN and chemicals.
  • Tracked, always. Clients individually track the loading and unloading of vehicles and view a huge variety of reports on our website — updated multiple times every day.
