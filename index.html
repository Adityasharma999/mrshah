(01) — Who we are

The pioneer at Kandla Complex.

Fig. 01 — M.R. Shah Chambers, Gandhidham

For six decades, M. R. Shah Logistics Pvt. Ltd. has been the trusted name in the transportation of essential commodities — petroleum products, LPG & other gases, edible and non-edible oils, ACN, organic & inorganic chemicals and other hazardous & non-hazardous products — ex-Kandla, Vadodara, Jamnagar and Surat to several parts of India.

A huge fleet of specialised liquid tankers, a vast human network and state-of-the-art infrastructure give us an edge over our competitors. Our head office runs up-to-date information and communication systems that keep management — and our clients — informed at all times.

  • ISO 9001 certified quality management systems
  • 300 owned and 500 attached vehicles in movement at any point of time
  • Safe, timely and complete delivery to any part of India
  • Liquid and dry cargo storage through group companies
