(01) — Our story

Proudly the pioneer at Kandla.

Fig. 01 — M.R. Shah Chambers, Gandhidham

M. R. Shah Logistics Pvt. Ltd. feels proud to call itself the pioneer in transportation based at Kandla Complex for the last several decades — engaged in the transportation of essential commodities like petroleum products, LPG & other gases, edible / non-edible oil, ACN, organic & inorganic chemicals and other hazardous & non-hazardous products ex-Kandla, Vadodara, Jamnagar and Surat to several parts of India.

We have a huge fleet of specialised liquid tankers to carry specific chemicals, and we ensure safe, timely and complete delivery to any part of India. A vast human network and state-of-the-art infrastructure give us an extra edge over our competitors; our head office is equipped with a very sound and up-to-date information and communication system which keeps the management well-informed at all times.

“We believe in a win-win situation — winning ourselves as well as letting our respectable clients win through our efficient services and fulfilment of their each and every requirement. For us, winning is to win people and their trust.”The Inner Strength — M. R. Shah Group

(03) — Mission & merits

What drives us, what proves us.

Group’s mission

  • To win our customers’ trust by providing them utmost satisfaction.
  • To make continuous improvements in rendering quality services, adhering to stringent safety measures keeping human safety in mind.
  • To motivate our workforce to work with complete dedication towards the common goal of taking the company to new heights.
  • To protect our workforce and environment from the hazards of handling LPG & other gases, edible & non-edible oils, ACN and organic & inorganic chemicals by complying with all safety codes and statutory requirements laid down by the Central & State Governments.
  • To conduct safety audits, meetings, surveys, awareness and training programmes on a regular basis.

Our merits

  • An ISO 9001:2000 certified company.
  • Lifting 6,50,000 tonnes (6.5 lakh MT) of cargo per annum.
  • Fleet of 300 owned and 500 attached vehicles in movement at any point of time.
  • Expertise in handling complex movements with complete safety & security of hazardous and non-hazardous goods.
  • Catering to the specific requirements of customers engaged in diverse industries.
  • Providing storage facilities for liquid and dry cargo.
  • Providing online reports on the company’s website, updated several times every single day.

(04) — Milestones

1966 onwards.

A journey from M. R. Transport’s first truck to a sophisticated, technology-driven logistics group.

1966

The functioning of M. R. Transport commenced.

1986

The count of owned vehicles crossed 100.

1988

Introduced ACN tanks.

1994

Introduced LPG tanks.

1995

The company’s first branch started in Vadodara.

1996

Introduced stainless steel tanks.

1998

Group company Agencies & Cargo Care commenced.

2002

Started using customised software supporting a variety of reports for better management.

2004

Kandla Warehouse & Logistics Pvt. Ltd. started functioning.

2007

The company’s revolutionary, full-fledged and sophisticated website with online tracking was launched.
